Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of 2248: Number Merge Puzzle Game can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Send an email to developerpjohn@gmail.com with the subject 2248 Account Deletion Request.

Please include the email address associated with your App account so that we can identify the account to be deleted.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize personal account information and account-associated game data that we control, subject to information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.

Google Play transaction records are maintained by Google and are subject to Google's retention policies. Payment card and bank-account information is handled by Google Play and is not stored by the App.

Processing time

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period and will contact you if additional information is required to verify the request.
